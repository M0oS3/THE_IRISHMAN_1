Knowledge Desk

RSI · momentum

Compares recent gains and losses. Readings above 70 or below 30 can persist in strong trends.

EMA · trend

Weights recent prices more heavily. Crossovers lag and can produce false signals in sideways markets.

MACD · momentum shift

Compares fast and slow averages. Signal-line crosses describe momentum, not certainty.

Bollinger bands · dispersion

Typically a moving average plus/minus two standard deviations. Band touches do not guarantee reversals.

Volatility · movement

Recent candle ranges describe past movement, not future risk. Gaps and fast moves can exceed expectations.

Backtesting · evidence

Include costs, compare a baseline, test unseen periods, and avoid tuning a strategy repeatedly to one sample.
